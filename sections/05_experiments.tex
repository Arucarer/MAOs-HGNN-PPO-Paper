\section{Experimental Results and Discussion}
%【章节标题译文】
% 实验结果与讨论
\label{sec:experiments}

%【第 5 节｜第 01 段｜5-P01】
The experiments evaluate whether the state representation in Section~\ref{sec:method} improves adaptive
rule selection for multi-level processing--assembly scheduling. The analysis proceeds from dataset construction
and parameter selection to component ablation, instance-wise performance, and independent testing.
Makespan and schedule deviation assess production efficiency and plan stability, while hypervolume
and spread describe the quality of the resulting objective trade-offs.

% 实验围绕状态编码和规则选择展开，不扩展为规则演化方法的贡献验证。分别从效率、稳定性及双目标解集质量分析，

medium, and large groups. Each scale includes ten training instances, denoted by TR\_S01--TR\_S10,
TR\_M01--TR\_M10, and TR\_L01--TR\_L10, and five test instances, denoted by S\_01--S\_05, M\_01--M\_05,

nominal durations, and multi-level assembly requirements. Differences in scale change both resource competition
and the number of interacting assembly branches.

SPT-M compares processing durations, whereas ECT-M includes feasible-start waiting; EAM uses
start time alone. Queued workload must be defined consistently without double-counting unassigned
operations. BAM requires a specified fallback when no baseline machine is available, including for
new orders. Sequencing durations refer to the machine currently making the sequencing decision.
MWKR requires an explicit remaining-work convention. MSLK requires a documented target completion
time; a baseline target may be used only for tasks with a baseline record, with a specified fallback
for new orders. BAS-O preserves baseline order heuristically; it does not directly minimize the
start-time and assignment deviation in Eq.~\eqref{eq:objective_deviation}.

Recovery rules compare feasible recovery plans, not merely machine labels. EAR includes waiting
and rerouting; ALT-ECT restricts its primary comparison to alternative machines. With no feasible
alternative, the environment forces waiting as in Section~\ref{subsec:mathematical_model}.
Completion estimates must use the full updated restart duration and only revealed repair information;
unknown repair completion times require an explicit convention. MIN-DEV requires a defined estimate
of schedule deviation, rather than claiming knowledge of the final objective. ACR-M, ACR-O, and
ASM-REC require observable assembly-readiness estimates and executable scoring definitions.
In particular, a criticality score constant across a routing operation's machine choices would not
distinguish those choices; its decision behavior must be checked against ECT-M.

All rules require deterministic tie breaking, feasibility handling, and missing-input conventions
before evaluation. Compare their decoded decisions on common feasible states to identify redundancy;
report disagreement rates on states where both rules apply, together with two-objective performance.
A conditional ranking alone need not capture complementary behavior under the learned selector.

\subsection{Discussion}
%【章节标题译文】
% 讨论
\label{subsec:experimental_discussion}

%【第 5.6 节｜第 01 段｜5.6-P01】
The results provide complementary views of the state encoder. Parameter analysis identifies a common
configuration for the comparisons. Attention ablation shows lower late-training objective curves
for the complete configuration, particularly on the larger groups. The instance-wise tables show
that this advantage is more consistent for makespan than for deviation. Independent testing further
shows favorable average solution-set coverage, while individual cases reveal limits in extreme trade-offs
and distribution uniformity.

and assembly information before selecting a dispatching rule. Role-specific readout then exposes
